Who am I?

I'm Melika! A UX Designer and Researcher based with a background in psychology. I specialize in creating user-centered digital experiences by combining rigorous research with thoughtful design. My work spans enterprise and fintech environments, where I focus on improving usability, user satisfaction, trust, and product experience.

I've conducted global usability studies, quantitative analyses, and strategic product mapping at SAP, and led end-to-end research and design initiatives for B2B payment and escrow products at Kutana Technologies.
