Floo Pay

UX Research | UX Design | Key Takeaways

✕ ✕

Overview: Kutana Pay is a cross-border payment platform where users can create wallets, deposit and withdraw money in different currencies, convert between currencies, and add recipients.

Role: UX Researcher, UX/UI designer

Toolkit: Figma, FigJam, Notability

Test Prototype

UX Research

• Background:

Many users struggle with hidden conversion fees, disorganized transactions, and the complexity of tracking income from different sources. Users need a transparent, user-friendly platform for handling transactions, currency conversion, and wallet organization.

• Research Goal:

understand the pain points and needs of users managing multiple currencies and transactions to design and create a seamless experience that enhances control over personal and business finances.

• Methodologies:

  1. User Interviews
  2. Wish cards & Thinking-Aloud

User Interviews

• Overview:

Semi-structured qualitative interviews via Zoom and in-person with 8 participants (Freelancers, Business Owners, and Remote Workers), who manage finances across multiple accounts and currencies.

• Affinity Mapping:

Multi currency management
"I don't know how much I'm losing in currency conversion. It feels like I always get less money than expected."
"Why can't I see the exchange rate breakdown before making a transfer? Hidden fees make me anxious."
"Sometimes the exchange rate is different from what I see online, and I don't understand why."
"I wish I could lock in a good exchange rate before making a transfer."
Business & Personal separation of Finances
"I mix up my personal and work transactions all the time. I need a better way to separate them."
"At the end of the month, I struggle to filter out which transactions are business-related for tax purposes."
"I need a way to export business expenses for reimbursement easily."
Transaction History & Search
"Scrolling through past transactions is a nightmare. I can't find what I need without wasting time."
"I wish I could search for transactions by category, not just by date."
"It's frustrating when I don't remember the exact amount or date of a transaction, so I can't find it easily."
"I'd like to tag certain transactions as important so I can find them later."
User Control & Transparency
"I wish I could review the exact breakdown of what I'm paying before I confirm a transaction."
"I feel like I'm always being charged unexpected fees, and I don't know why."

Research Findings

Managing multiple currencies is frustrating!

Need for transparent conversion fees.

Business vs. Personal Expenses Need Separation

A multi-wallet system is crucial.

Transaction History Needs Better Filters & Search

Users struggle to find past transactions.

High Fees and Hidden Charges are a Concern

A fee transparency breakdown is necessary.

Persona #1

Maria Gonzalez

38 y/o

💼 Owner of an online boutique

📍 Barcelona, Spain

Frequently-used tools
Bio

Maria is the owner of an online boutique in Barcelona, Spain. With a degree in Business Administration and International Trade, she has always been passionate about helping small businesses expand globally. Her experience as an export consultant made her aware of the challenges of cross-border payments, exchange rates, and financial management. As her boutique grew, she struggled to track business expenses, manage multiple currencies, and avoid hidden fees.

Goals
  • Manage business expenses and profits in a structured way
  • Easily send and receive payments from international customers
  • Convert currencies efficiently without losing money on hidden fees
Pain points
  • Exchange rate fluctuations impact her profits unpredictably
  • Complicated transaction histories make financial tracking a challenge
  • No clear way to categorize business vs. personal expenses

Bio

Ahmed Rahman is a remote software engineer who works remotely with a background in Computer Science from the University of Toronto. His lifestyle requires a seamless financial solution that can adapt to his nomadic needs without excessive fees.

Pain points

  • No easy way to set financial goals and monitor savings progress

UX Design

Prioritization

• Project Goals:

While Business goals were mainly focused in gaining as much users as possible by making clear how the platform works, I had a deeper vision of what users wanted and I was able to identify where the two of them aligned.

Wireframes

According to the project goals, I sketched 4 main pages for dashboard, wallets, covert, and transactions. Considering the goals of my personas, the end user should be able to complete their task flows in these 4 categories.

Design system

I have created a colour pallet with 1 primary colour as well as 2 secondary colour, along with 3 neutral ones. As for Typography, I decided to go with Inter for better legibility.

Key Takeaways

• Challenges:

- To find the right angle together with an interesting story to tell when dealing with a widely explored industry such as Fintech.

- A huge effort in prioritisation when finding the starting point.

- To think about detailed aspects users might need interacting with a FinPlatform without getting overwhelmed.

• Lessons learned:

- The effort of keeping it simple can make a difference in the final outcome, starting form wireframes up to the prototype experience.

- The importance of a well-designed UI library, that is built up using components, variants and frames.

- Designing in a consistent way can truly help speed up the whole process.
