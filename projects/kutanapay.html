Kutana Pay

UX Research | UX Design | Key Takeaways

✕ ✕ ✕

Overview: A digital financial management platform designed to help users track their income, expenses, transactions, and wallets while offering currency conversion services.

Role: UX Researcher, UX/UI designer

Toolkit: Figma, FigJam, Notability, ConfigCat

See it live

UX Research

• Background:

To enable Freelancers and SMEs trade securely there was a need for the platform to be redesigned to a more seamless experience to manage their finance. The redesign aimed to accommodate updated features and bring a user-friendly experience with the platform, putting users as centres of the study.

• Research Goal:

Identify persona types, understand the pain points and needs of users managing multiple currencies and transactions to redesign the platform to acomplish enhanced control over personal and business finances.

• Methodologies:

  1. Contextual Inquiry
  2. Usability Testing
  3. Validation with PostHog

User Interviews

• Overview:

Semi-structured qualitative interviews via MS Teams with 8 participants (Freelancers, Business Owners) as primary users of the platform.

• Contextual Inquiry:

By watching the users and listening as they explain their usual tasks using the platform, I pieced together a more holistic understanding of the problem to ultimately create a larger solution space to solve the pain points of the users.

Research Findings

Unclear conversion and transaction fees

Need for transparency when it comes to fees.

Confusion with tracking financial activity per currency

A multi-wallet system is crucial.

Transaction History Needs Better Filters & Search

Users struggle to find past transactions.

High Fees and Hidden Charges are a Concern

A fee transparency breakdown is necessary.

Freelance UX Designer

Needs

  • ✅Low fees and transparent conversion rates
  • ✅A platform that guarantees she’ll get paid once her work is done
  • ✅Simple tools to track, withdraw, and manage her freelance income

How Kutana Pay helps?

  • Instant or same-day payouts directly to her mobile money or local bank account
  • Option to hold funds in USD or GHS, and withdraw when it’s best for her
  • Low, transparent transaction fees = more money in her pocket
  • Digital escrow protection ensures she’s paid for every completed job
  • Easy-to-use wallet and transaction dashboard — no more guessing or waiting

Business Owner Persona #1

Lebo Mokoena

34 y/o

Chief Operating Officer

Nairobi, Kenya

Biography

Lebo is the co-founder and operations lead at TalentNest, a platform that connects Africa's top freelance professionals with global clients. She manages the operations team, oversees freelancer onboarding, ensures project delivery standards, and most critically, handles payments between clients and freelancers. With rapid growth and freelancers in over 10 African countries, she's always looking for a more efficient, affordable, and trustworthy way to move money across borders.

Pain points

  • ✕Delays and inconsistencies in paying freelancers across Africa
  • ✕High banking fees and exchange losses, eating into platform margins and freelancer earnings

  • ✅Fast, low-cost cross-border payments with support for local currencies
  • ✅A built-in digital escrow feature for trust and milestone-based work

  • ✅Support for various payout methods (bank, card, wallet) without complexity

How Kutana Pay helps?

  • Fast, reliable global transactions — freelancers are paid as soon as work is approved
  • Flat 1.5% fee helps TalentNest preserve margins and increase freelancer satisfaction
  • Multi-currency wallets for both international (USD, EUR) and African currencies
  • Built-in escrow functionality ensures payment security and builds trust between freelancers and clients
  • All-in-one dashboard to manage payouts, currencies, and payment status across multiple countries
  • Supports mobile money and local banks, meeting freelancers where they are

Business Owner Persona #2

Jean-Michel Lefevre

38 y/o

Charter Sales & Client Services Manager

Nice, France (with regular travel to London and Dubai)

Biography

Jean-Michel Lefevre, 38, is the Charter Sales & Client Services Manager at Farringdon Jets, based in Nice, France. He handles high-value bookings across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa, often under tight deadlines. His main challenges are delayed payments, currency issues, and lack of real-time payment tracking. Kutana Pay helps him confirm bookings faster with low-fee, multi-currency transfers and instant payment visibility.

Pain points

  • ✕ Client Payments Delay Flight Confirmations:

    Jean-Michel often needs client payments confirmed quickly to secure aircraft availability, but traditional payment methods are slow, especially for last-minute bookings involving African or Gulf region banks.

  • ✕ FX and Cross-Border Confusion:

    Clients might pay in AED, EUR, or USD, but local service providers require payments in local African currencies. Currency mismatches and poor exchange rates cause confusion and even payment rejections.

  • ✕ Lack of Visibility for International Transfers:

    When a client says they've paid, Jean-Michel has no real-time visibility into whether the funds have arrived, causing delays in approvals and undermining client trust.

  • ✕ High Fees Impact Budget-Sensitive Flights:

    Some bookings are price-sensitive, and Jean-Michel struggles to keep costs down when bank fees and FX margins inflate transfer amounts — especially when working with clients paying from multiple jurisdictions.

Needs

  • ✅ Fast Payment Confirmation:

    A way to instantly see when a payment is received so flights can be confirmed without delay.

  • ✅ Multi-Currency Support with Fair FX Rates:

    The ability to accept and send funds across multiple currencies seamlessly, without losing money to poor exchange rates or needing multiple bank accounts.

  • ✅ Client-Friendly Payment Options:

    A smooth, intuitive way for clients to send money securely and quickly, even when booking last minute.

  • ✅ Transparent Fees and Real-Time Tracking:

    Full clarity into what was charged, how much reached the recipient, and where a transfer is at any moment.

How Kutana Pay helps?

  • Instant Payment Visibility: Kutana Pay gives Jean-Michel real-time payment updates, allowing him to confirm bookings faster and reassure clients that everything is on track.
  • Cross-Border Currency Flexibility: With Kutana's multi-currency wallets and fair FX rates, he can manage international payments with ease and ensure local partners are paid in the correct currency without hidden losses.
  • Client-Friendly Interface: Kutana's payment system is simple and intuitive for clients, making it easy for them to send deposits even on tight timelines — especially important in VIP charter services.
  • Low Fees and Full Transparency: Jean-Michel can quote clients confidently, knowing there won't be any surprise charges or payment delays, helping him deliver smoother, trust-based service.

UX Design

Prioritization

• Project Goals:

While Business goals were mainly focused in gaining as much customers as possible by making clear how the platform works, I had a deeper vision of what users wanted and I was able to identify where the two of them aligned.

Wireframes

According to the project goals, I sketched the main pages for dashboard, wallets, covert, transactions, notifications and recipients. Considering the goals of the main personas, the end user should be able to complete their task flows in the 6 menu items.

User flows

I then identified the main user flows such as signing up, completing KYC/KYB forms, getting support, making a deposit, withdrawal, and conversion per different wallet respecting different compliant requirements.

Design system

Key Takeaways

• Challenges:

- Adhering to regional financial regulations (GDPR, PSD2, KYC/AML) while maintaining design consistency.

- Building trust in a digital financial environment where users cannot see or interact physically with their money or advisors.

- Ensuring usability across demographics including unbanked or low-digital-literacy users.

• Lessons learned:

- Tiny feedback moments such as real-time validations make complex financial actions feel safe.

- Designing consent screens and privacy dashboards became opportunities to build trust through transparency.

- Iterative feedback loops with developers and QA helped identify edge cases (e.g., failed transactions, API delays). This taught me to prototype realistically, anticipating system states and errors.
